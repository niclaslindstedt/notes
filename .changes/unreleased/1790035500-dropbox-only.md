---
type: Removed
title: Google Drive sync
---

Google Drive is no longer one of the places notes can be kept. This browser, a
folder you pick, Dropbox and a Nextcloud you run yourself remain.

If you had Drive connected, the app falls back to this browser. Your notes stay
in their app folder in your own Drive, where you can read, export or delete
them as you would any other file — nothing is removed from your account.
