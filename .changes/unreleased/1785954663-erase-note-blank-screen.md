---
type: Fixed
title: Erasing a whole note no longer blanks the app
---

Erasing everything in a note — whether by iOS's "Select All" from the text-selection callout, or by backspacing down to the empty "start writing" prompt and pressing it once more — no longer crashes the app to a black screen that only a cold restart clears, and any crash that does slip through now offers a reload button instead of a blank page.
