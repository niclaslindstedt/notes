type: Added
title: Multiple cursors
---

Edit at several places at once on a computer: ⌘/Ctrl+D selects the word under the cursor and then each next occurrence of it, ⌘/Ctrl+↑ / ↓ grow a column of cursors a line at a time, every keystroke lands at all of them — copy, cut and paste included, a column with nothing selected taking whole lines the way VS Code does — and Escape drops back to one.
